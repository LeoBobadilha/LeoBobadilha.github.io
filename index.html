02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

About my
approach.

I'm a Malta-based data and business intelligence professional working with SQL Server, Python, SSIS and Power BI across production reporting and data workflows.

My day-to-day work involves tracing data across source and reporting layers, validating transformations, investigating inconsistencies and helping turn data into dependable information.

Beyond that, I use independent projects to explore new domains, challenge assumptions and develop practical, well-documented systems.
